Deploy GraveyardRaffle on Base

Run from the repo root with python -m http.server 8120 after forge build, then open http://localhost:8120/tools/deploy.html in the browser with your wallet. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

Deploys the raffle with Chainlink VRF v2.5 wrapper 0xb0407dbe851f8318bd31404A49e658143C982F23 and Zombies On Base 0xa2d33352e3e2b4fe1b8af7387c375828197bcd9b. The deploying wallet becomes the owner.
